# Cover letter draft for author completion

Dear Editor,

Please consider the manuscript “Hierarchical RF Tokenization for
Parameter-Efficient Cross-Receiver LoRa Identification.” The paper examines
token formation within a fixed, strictly in-band LoRa fingerprinting pipeline.
Its central result is a hierarchical tokenizer that uses substantially fewer
parameters than a full patch projection and improves the existing development
protocol's receiver-equal accuracy. Parameter-matched controls and targeted
interventions support the design while delimiting its interpretation.

The paper reports the less favorable evidence as well: higher counted MACs and
recorded latency than the matched patch control, substantial digital-shift
sensitivity, and default-precision BN-folding discrepancies. Development and
historical-confirmation receivers have prior exposure; the manuscript does not
present them as a new blind test or claim an exhaustive state-of-the-art result.

We disclose the related author-supplied manuscript “Diagnosing Receiver-Sensitive
Out-of-Band Dependencies in LoRa Radio-Frequency Fingerprint Identification.”
The corpus, strictly in-band preprocessing, five-channel front-end and token
interface are inherited. The present study asks a different question about
token construction, matched parameter budgets and deployment transformations,
without an OOB branch. A component-level relationship statement is supplied.

The supplementary material includes the receiver protocol, complete derived
tables, conditional comparisons, precision checks and evidence provenance.
We would appreciate an assessment of the manuscript within these stated limits.

Sincerely,

The submitting authors
