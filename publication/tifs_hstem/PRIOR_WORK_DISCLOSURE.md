# Relationship to the companion LoRa study

The related manuscript supplied to the project is “Diagnosing Receiver-Sensitive Out-of-Band Dependencies in LoRa Radio-Frequency Fingerprint Identification,” by Ziyang Wang, Chengcheng Han, Jianhuan Chen, and Xin Zhang. The corresponding author should supply its verified publication or submission record and the review copy required by the journal.

The two studies share the public multi-receiver LoRa corpus, RMS normalization, strictly in-band reconstruction, five views, the kernel-7/kernel-5 local front end, and the 32-by-64 token interface. Receiver sets used for development, historical confirmation, and the companion's six-receiver blind evaluation retain their documented prior use.

The companion studies dependence on receiver-sensitive out-of-band information using pathway interventions. The present work focuses on the tokenizer after the strictly in-band front end. Its contributions are the four-stage HStem construction at a fixed interface, parameter- and support-matched direct projections under the current LoRa protocol, and audits of the factors that contribute to the tokenizer's performance. Comparisons of architectural effects use paired arms within the current protocol.
